Our technology

One membrane platform. Different separation targets.

Instead of offering a single fixed membrane for every process, SCELTAMEM is positioned as a selective-membrane development platform. The target ion, competing ions, concentration range, pH, temperature and process architecture can guide membrane selection and optimization.

Target selection

Li⁺, Mg²⁺, K⁺, Na⁺ and beyond

Membrane development is oriented toward the ion or ion pair that matters most for the customer's process.

Feed-specific

Designed around real streams

Brines, geothermal waters, recycling streams and other complex mixtures can require different selectivity strategies.

Process-ready

From material to separation step

Our approach connects membrane properties with transport, operating conditions, module requirements and scale-up needs.
